04What happens after you sign

  1. Your Softwash + Tune-Up agreement

    You receive your Softwash + Tune-Up agreement with Roof Logix FL and sign it.

  2. Soft wash and tune-up

    Roof Logix FL performs the soft wash and the tune-up on your roof.

  3. Formal inspection and qualification

    Before the treatment contract, a formal inspection is completed and the licensed partner contractor confirms your roof qualifies for the treatment.

  4. Your GoNano treatment contract — separately

    You receive the GoNano treatment contract from the installing licensed contractor company, separately, to review and sign before any application.

  5. Application and warranty registration

    The licensed contractor performs the application, and the GoNano warranty is registered in your name within 30 days.

Nothing moves forward to the treatment without your signed treatment contract at that stage. Until then, you may cancel this engagement in writing, free, at any time.
